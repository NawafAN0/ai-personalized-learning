## Research Overview

This project answers the question:
> *"How does the Personalized Learning industry use AI and Generative AI today?"*

**Topics covered:**
- Predictive AI vs Generative AI in education
- Deep learning models: Transformer, LLM, Deep Knowledge Tracing (DKT)
- Case studies: Khan Academy (Khanmigo), Duolingo (Birdbrain), Coursera (Coach)
- Challenges: bias, student data privacy, EU AI Act
- Future outlook for AI in education (2025–2030)
